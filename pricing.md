# Pricing — Plien Roob (เปลี่ยนรูป)

AI product photography for Thai online shops. https://plienroob.com

All prices include VAT and are charged in Thai baht on the Thai site.
The English site charges the same plans in US dollars.

## Free

- Price: ฿0 — one time, no card required
- Credits: 10 once
- Model photos: about 5 (a try-on image costs 2 credits)
- The first image has no watermark; later output carries one and cannot be used for selling
- Watermark: yes, except the first image

## Basic (เบสิก)

- Price: ฿590/month | ฿5,280/year (฿440/month equivalent)
- Credits: 180 per month
- Model photos: about 90 (a try-on image costs 2 credits)
- Cost per model photo: ฿6.56 monthly, ฿4.89 on the yearly plan
- Watermark: no

## Premium (พรีเมียม)

- Price: ฿1,290/month | ฿11,640/year (฿970/month equivalent)
- Credits: 465 per month
- Model photos: about 232 (a try-on image costs 2 credits)
- Cost per model photo: ฿5.56 monthly, ฿4.18 on the yearly plan
- Watermark: no

## Credit top-ups

Available to shops that already have a monthly plan. One-time payment, no subscription.
Paid by PromptPay QR.

- Small: ฿350 — 100 credits on Basic, 105 credits on Premium
- Medium: ฿700 — 200 credits on Basic, 250 credits on Premium
- Large: ฿1,400 — 400 credits on Basic, 530 credits on Premium

## What a credit buys

Credits are spent per generated image, and each tool costs a different amount.
**One credit is not one image.**

- Virtual try-on (ลองสวมใส่): from 2 credits per image
- Product to model (เปลี่ยนสินค้า): from 2 credits per image
- Ghost mannequin (หุ่นล่องหน): from 2 credits per image
- Upscale (ขยายภาพ): from 1 credit per image
- Background removal (ลบพื้นหลัง): from 1 credit per image
- Product Showcase (พรีเซนต์สินค้า): from 3 credits per image (2K)
- Product-Sexy: 3 credits per image (2K only)

Some tools offer a higher-quality tier that costs more credits per image.
The exact cost is shown on screen before every generation.

## Payment methods

- Monthly and yearly plans: credit or debit card only. PromptPay cannot charge on a
  recurring schedule, which is a limitation of PromptPay itself, not a policy choice.
- Credit top-ups: PromptPay QR.

## Rules that affect cost

- Credits never expire.
- A failed generation refunds its credits automatically and in full.
- The free plan gives 10 credits once. It is not renewed.
